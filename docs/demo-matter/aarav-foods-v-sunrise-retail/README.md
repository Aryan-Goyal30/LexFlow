# Demonstration matter: Aarav Foods Pvt. Ltd. v. Sunrise Retail Pvt. Ltd. (fictional)

**Illustrative academic material.** The parties, marks, events, figures and
letters are invented. The legal authorities are genuine Supreme Court of India
judgments from LexFlow's verified corpus (`docs/verification/batch2/`). Nothing
here is a filing, an official record or legal advice.

The documents are generated by `server/scripts/make_aarav_demo_pack.py`.
`server/tests/test_prepared_analysis.py` checks that every sentence the
prepared analysis quotes is in the text LexFlow extracts from them.

## The dispute in brief

- **Aarav Foods** (Pune; Delhi sales office since 2022) has sold baked millet
  snacks as **MILLETTA** since 1 August 2019. It comes in a bright yellow pack
  with a green band.
- **Sunrise Retail** (Gurugram; 64 Sunrise Fresh stores, 22 of them in Delhi)
  launched private-label **MILLETO** crisps on 20 January 2026, in a
  mustard-yellow pack with a green lower band and the SUNRISE house mark.
- **Correspondence:** a notice was sent on 2 March 2026, and Sunrise replied
  on 16 March 2026.
- **Client objective:** assess an interim injunction. The intended forum is
  the Delhi High Court, hypothetically.
- **Disputed:**
  - when Aarav learned of MILLETO: 3 February 2026 by Aarav's account,
    November 2025 by Sunrise's;
  - whether MILLETO is sold on quick-commerce apps.
- **Unverified:**
  - two customer complaints;
  - the MILLETTA registration (no e-register extract in the file).
