## Known limitations (kept by design)

- `ct-non-contradiction`: Kept: the validator proves both quotes are in the evidence, not that they conflict. Whether a contradiction is real is for the lawyer.
- `oc-paraphrase`: Kept: outcome wording is caught by patterns; paraphrases pass.
- `pc-one-statement-pair`: Kept: different dates for different events are not a conflict, but validation checks provenance only; the prompt (v2) tells the model not to report these, and the lawyer judges what is shown.
